Quarterly Progress Update - Q3 2025

The team has made significant progress on the distributed computing framework this quarter.
Key achievements include implementing the new consensus algorithm, reducing latency by 23%,
and onboarding three new team members. The machine learning pipeline was refactored to support
batch processing of up to 10 million records per hour. Next quarter, the focus will shift to
improving fault tolerance and expanding the API surface for external integrations.

Budget utilization stands at 78% of the annual allocation. The remaining funds are earmarked
for hardware upgrades and cloud infrastructure scaling planned for Q4.
